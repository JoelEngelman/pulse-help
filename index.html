NEW TO PULSE?

Start with the basics.

Follow the quick path from account creation to your first message.

  1. Create or sign into your Pulse account.
  2. Set up your profile.
  3. Find another Pulse account.
  4. Open a conversation and send a message.

Account & profile

Click any question. The answer expands right underneath it.

Use Pulse's account creation screen and complete the requested fields. If an error appears, refresh once and retry rather than repeatedly submitting the form.

Sign out of the current account, then sign into the other account. If your Pulse build has an account switcher, use it to move between available sessions.

Open account settings, choose the password option, enter the required information and save your new password.

Pulse should not reveal an existing password in plain text. A visibility control may only reveal what you are currently typing.

Open your profile, edit the information you want to change and save. Refresh if the updated information does not appear immediately.

Check the account name and password, check Caps Lock, refresh Pulse and retry. If the account does not exist, create it first.

Never share your password. Pulse support will not need your password to troubleshoot an account.

Messaging

Everything about sending and receiving messages.

  1. Open Messaging.
  2. Choose the recipient.
  3. Type your message.
  4. Press Send.

Confirm you are signed into the expected account, refresh the conversation and verify that the recipient account exists.

Make sure both accounts exist, you're using the correct account, and your connection is working. Then refresh and retry.

Conversations are designed to work in both directions. If one side cannot see messages, refresh both sessions and check the connection.

Troubleshooting

Open a problem to reveal its fix.

  1. Refresh the page.
  2. Check your internet connection.
  3. Try another browser.
  4. If you're on a managed network, check whether it is restricting Pulse.

Don't repeatedly submit account creation. Refresh and retry once. If the same database error continues, record the exact error and report it.

Verify the recipient exists, confirm the current account, refresh the conversation and check the network connection.

School & restricted networks

Network questions, with answers that actually open.

Managed networks can restrict domains or services. Pulse uses normal web-friendly HTTPS endpoints, but the network can still apply its own access rules.

Yes. A current Edge, Chrome, Safari or Firefox browser can help determine whether an issue is browser-specific.

Check whether other normal websites work. If Pulse alone is blocked, a network administrator may need to change the policy.

Privacy & safety

Simple rules for keeping your Pulse account safe.

Use a strong password, don't share it and avoid reusing an important password from another service.

Before sending something private, check the account name and conversation shown in the messaging screen.

Include the exact error message and what you were doing when it happened. Never include your password or private credentials.
